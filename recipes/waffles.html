Homemade Belgian Waffles

A simple waffle recipe with a crisp exterior, a soft centre, and your favourite toppings.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 15 min Total: 25 min Easy

Ingredients

  • 250 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 300 ml milk
  • 75 g melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Fresh strawberries and blueberries, for serving

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
  3. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients. Stir until just combined; avoid overmixing.
  4. Preheat your waffle maker and lightly grease it according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  5. Pour enough batter into the waffle maker to cover the cooking surface without overflowing.
  6. Cook until the waffles are golden and crisp. Cooking time depends on your waffle maker.
  7. Serve warm with fresh strawberries, blueberries, or your favourite toppings.
