Homemade Creamy Risotto

Take your time with this comforting dish. Gradually adding warm stock helps create its signature creamy texture.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 25 min Total: 35 min Medium

Ingredients

  • 200 g Arborio rice
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 25 g butter
  • 750 ml warm vegetable stock
  • 50 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley, optional

Instructions

  1. Warm the vegetable stock in a saucepan and keep it hot over low heat.
  2. Heat the olive oil and half the butter in a separate pan over medium-low heat.
  3. Add the chopped onion and cook gently until softened, without browning it.
  4. Add the Arborio rice and stir for 1–2 minutes until the grains are coated and lightly toasted.
  5. Add a ladleful of warm stock and stir. When most of the liquid has been absorbed, add another ladleful.
  6. Continue adding stock gradually and stirring regularly for about 18–25 minutes, until the rice is tender but still has a slight bite.
  7. Remove the pan from the heat. Stir in the remaining butter and Parmesan cheese.
  8. Season with salt and black pepper. Let the risotto rest briefly, then serve immediately with fresh parsley if desired.
