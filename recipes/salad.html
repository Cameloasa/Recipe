SIMPLE & REFRESHING

Easy Garden Salad

A quick everyday salad that works as a light lunch or a fresh side dish.

Prep: 15 min Cook: 0 min Total: 15 min Easy

Ingredients

  • 150 g mixed salad leaves
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 small red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 small bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh parsley, optional

Instructions

  1. Wash and dry the salad leaves and vegetables.
  2. Slice the cucumber, chop the tomatoes and bell pepper, and thinly slice the red onion.
  3. Place the salad leaves and prepared vegetables in a large bowl.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper.
  5. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss gently until the vegetables are lightly coated.
  6. Add fresh parsley if desired and serve immediately.
