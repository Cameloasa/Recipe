Fluffy Pancakes

Soft, golden pancakes made with simple ingredients, perfect for a relaxed breakfast or weekend brunch.

SIMPLE & DELICIOUS

Homemade Pancakes

A classic breakfast recipe with a soft texture and a golden finish.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 10 min Total: 20 min Easy

Ingredients

  • 150 g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • 1 egg
  • 180 ml milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter
  • Fresh berries and maple syrup, for serving

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, whisk the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir gently until combined. A few small lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered frying pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface, then flip and cook until golden.
  6. Repeat with the remaining batter. Serve warm with fresh berries and maple syrup.
