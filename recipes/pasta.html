Pasta with Tomato Sauce

An easy everyday meal made with familiar ingredients and plenty of flavour.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 20 min Total: 30 min Easy

Ingredients

  • 250 g pasta of your choice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh basil leaves, optional
  • Grated Parmesan cheese, for serving

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the garlic and cook briefly until fragrant, taking care not to burn it.
  4. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and dried oregano. Season with salt and black pepper.
  5. Let the sauce simmer gently for 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Drain the pasta, reserving a little pasta water. Add the pasta to the sauce and toss to combine.
  7. Add a splash of pasta water if needed. Serve with fresh basil and grated Parmesan.
