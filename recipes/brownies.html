Homemade Chocolate Brownies

Make these delicious brownies with simple ingredients. For the best texture, let them cool before slicing.

Prep: 10 min

Cook: 30 min

Total: 40 min

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 150 g unsalted butter
  • 200 g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 150 g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 75 g all-purpose flour
  • 25 g cocoa powder
  • A pinch of salt
  • 75 g chopped walnuts (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line a 20 cm square baking tin with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the butter and dark chocolate together over low heat or in short microwave intervals. Let the mixture cool slightly.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the sugar and eggs until combined. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  4. Add the melted chocolate mixture and stir until smooth.
  5. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Fold gently until just combined. Add the walnuts if desired.
  6. Pour the batter into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the edges are set and the centre is still slightly soft. Avoid overbaking.
  8. Let the brownies cool completely in the tin before cutting them into squares.
